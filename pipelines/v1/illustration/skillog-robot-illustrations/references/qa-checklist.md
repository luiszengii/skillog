# QA checklist

## Identity must pass

- The robot matches the bundled reference sheet and clearly remains the same model.
- Head and body are one integrated capsule with no neck or torso split.
- The face screen has exactly two white pill eyes and no added facial features.
- There is one ball-tip antenna, two ear pads, thin line limbs, dot hands, and flat oval feet.
- The violet-blue `S` appears on visible front surfaces only.
- Any visible rear shell is completely plain and unmarked.

## Scene must pass

- The robot performs the core conceptual action; removing it would break the metaphor.
- The image communicates one idea using no more than one or two main props.
- The composition has substantial clean white space and remains legible at post size.
- Text, if any, is short, necessary, correctly rendered, and not a title or paragraph.

## Style must pass

- Lines are thin, black, human, and slightly wobbly rather than vector-perfect.
- Looseness appears in line treatment, not in anatomy or identity drift.
- The image is clean and slightly strange, not cute, glossy, 3D, childish, or PPT-like.
- Violet is restrained; optional semantic colors do not compete with the robot.

## Iterate narrowly

- Wrong character structure: regenerate or edit while repeating every identity invariant.
- Too clean: increase only wobble, pressure variation, subtle retracing, and asymmetry.
- Too messy: reduce retracing and wobble without polishing into vector art.
- Too decorative: make the robot physically perform the concept.
- Too dense: remove props, labels, nodes, and background elements before changing the main metaphor.
- Wrong text: use a targeted text correction or regenerate with fewer labels.

Do not approve a visually attractive result that changes the robot's construction.
